The independent review rounds, the requests to colleagues and the pages are filed under Specification, standards and documentation, whatever their subject.

    Terms. LD: local damage (a broken wire, a short fault). LMA: loss of metallic area (steel lost over a length). B_rope: the magnetic induction in the rope; Br and Bz (or B_r): the radial and the axial field at a sensing element. lm: magnet length (thickness). Hcj: the magnet's resistance to demagnetisation; the knee: the point past which a magnet loses strength for good. Hard and easy wire: rope steel that magnetises with difficulty or easily (the two ends of the range the models assume). Hall, TMR, GMR, AMR: kinds of magnetic sensing element. Basis "calculated": a model result; "measured": from the prototype recording; "data sheet": read from a maker's data sheet; no basis: counts, readings of standards and review answers.

      How to update this page

      The page holds no plan of its own. It reads two files that sit next to it, plan.json and record.json, every time it opens. The files are kept in the repository fjordbit/max, folder docs/sws: edit them there (the web editor of the repository is enough) and save; nothing has to be built. An edit made there reaches this page at the next release of the pages, not at once. A wrong edit does not break the page silently: it lists the problem in a red box at the top. For a check on a computer, run python docs/sws/check_plan_data.py in that repository: it prints ok or the problems.

      plan.json: one entry per task in tasks

      id
      a whole number, used once (1 to 20: the schedule; 21 to 100: added; 101 and up: work done)
      area
      one of the ids in areas: (the list in plan.json)
      task
      one line: what is to be done
      lead
      who leads the task, first names only, several separated by " / "; a group may be named as a group; "To name" when nobody is named yet
      start, end
      dates written YYYY-MM-DD, start not after end
      state
      Not started, In progress, Completed
      progress
      a whole number from 0 to 100 (100 when the state is Completed)
      priority
      Critical, High, Medium, Low; Critical tasks carry a red bar on the left
      depends_on, risk
      free text; may be empty ("")

      Also in plan.json: updated (the date of your edit, YYYY-MM-DD: change it every time; the page counts the open tasks past their end date against it and shows it at the bottom), dates (the controlling dates: when, what, state; the first one is shown as the next controlling date, so take a date out once it has passed), needed (the decisions and inputs needed, one sentence each) and areas (an id and a name each; a new area needs an id before a task can use it).

      record.json

      work
      everything done, one entry per piece of work: area (as above), topic (rows with the same topic are grouped), when, what (what was done), result (what it showed, figures with their units), basis (calculated, measured, data sheet, or empty); optional review_round (true for an independent review round) and merged (a note when two rows were joined)
      open
      since and item
      period
      the span the record covers, shown in the "In short" box

      Plain words for readers outside the daily work. Anything not yet settled is "proposed", never "selected" or "decided". No last names anywhere; first names only in the lead field of a task, nowhere else. No customer or site names: the first installation is "the first installation (38 mm rope)", the large one "the large installation (52 mm rope)".
