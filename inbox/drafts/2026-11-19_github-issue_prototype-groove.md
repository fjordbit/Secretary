Title: SWS prototype head: dimensions of the groove in the yoke (and of the cut-out that did not help)

Body:

**Why.** The model now shows that a groove all round the outside of the yoke, at the sensors, sets the distance
from the rope where the standing axial field is zero. The depth of the groove decides that distance: about 0.4 mm
per 0.1 mm of depth. We want to check the model against the prototype head, and for that we need the real
dimensions. We have not received them.

**Task.** For the 40 mm prototype head, please give:

1. The groove that helped: width, depth, and position along the yoke (centred on the sensor plane or not).
   A drawing, a sketch or a photo with a ruler is enough.
2. The yoke wall thickness at the groove and outside it, and the yoke steel grade if known.
3. The earlier cut-out that did not help: where it was (inside or outside of the yoke), width and depth.
4. The radius or stand-off of the axial and the radial sensors on the board.

**Done when** the dimensions are written in this issue or a drawing is attached to it.
