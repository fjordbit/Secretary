**Subject:** Enquiry: 52 mm reference rope with defined faults, for verification of magnetic rope testing (MRT) equipment

Hello,

We use your 52 mm Powerplast rope on our shiplifts. We need a short reference rope of the same construction with
known, documented faults, to verify magnetic rope testing (MRT) equipment against EN 12927 and ISO 4309.

The attached list shows the faults we would like: single and multiple broken wires with a defined gap, in the outer
strands and in the inner rope, and four lengths with a known loss of metallic area (1, 5 and 10 %). The total length is about 24 m
between terminations.

Before we order, could you tell us:

1. Can you build broken wires with a defined gap into the rope during manufacture, also on wires that are hidden
   after closing? If not, which of the faults in the list can you make, and how?
2. How would you hold the gap (for example a non-magnetic filler), and what gap tolerance can you keep?
3. Can you make the loss of metallic area by leaving out or swapping wires over a defined length?
4. Is a short length like this possible, or is there a minimum production length? If there is, can the faults be
   placed in a part of a normal production run?
5. Can you document each fault as made: position, wire, gap, and a photo before it is covered?
6. Can you supply the certificate with the construction as made: wire diameters per layer, lay lengths, thickness
   of the plastic layer, wire grade, metallic area?
7. Price and lead time, for the full list and for the faults marked priority 1 only.

If some of the faults are not practical, we would like your advice on what is. A short call is welcome.
