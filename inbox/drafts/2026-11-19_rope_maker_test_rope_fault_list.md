# 52 mm reference rope with defined faults — fault list (attachment to the enquiry)

Rope: 52 mm Powerplast, same construction as delivered for shiplift service. Purpose: reference rope for
verification of magnetic rope testing (MRT) equipment (EN 12927, ISO 4309).

Source: branch u84-test-rope (4c8511c), `sws/procedures/test_rope_52mm_fault_list.md`, section 3. That list is a
first proposal, not merged and not checked by Kai. Internal columns and references are left out here.

## Words

- Crown wire: on the outside of an outer strand, visible.
- Valley wire: on the side of an outer strand, where two strands meet.
- Hidden wire: on the inner side of an outer strand, against the plastic layer.
- Inner-rope wire: in the steel inner rope, under the plastic layer.
- Gap: the distance between the two ends of a broken wire.
- Along: millimetres from the zero mark at end A.

## Layout

- 2.0 m of clean rope at each end, inside the terminations.
- At least 500 mm of clean rope between faults.
- Total about 22.1 m between terminations.
- Markers, all non-magnetic: a paint ring at the zero mark at end A, a straight paint line along the whole rope,
  a paint ring 100 mm before each fault, tags "END A" and "END B". No steel clamps, ties or tags.

## Wanted with the rope

- Certificate with the construction as made: lay type, direction and lengths, strands and wires per strand, wire
  diameters per layer, thickness of the plastic layer, wire grade, metallic area.
- For every fault as made: gap, wire and layer, number of wires, position from the zero mark, angle from the paint
  line, and a photo with a scale before the wire is covered.
- Positions of any wire welds or joints in the length, or a length without them.
- For L1–L3: number and size of the wires left out or swapped, and where each starts and ends.
- How the gaps are held open.
