# Ramin

**Role:** Stian's boss.

**Owns:** the parameterisation of Syncrolift's 3D models — he is its main
designer — and the VB application that drives it.

## What he built

CATIA was chosen precisely because absolutely everything in the models is a
parameter that can be changed from outside. Ramin's VB macros and scripts sit on
top of that: change a variable, and the model rebuilds around it. Stian's
example was wire rope thickness.

Stian's assessment, in his words: it is **good, but highly engineered**. He sees
a later job in rewriting it into a more modern framework — more efficient,
easier for someone new to pick up, with proper help documentation out of the box
rather than knowledge held in the code and in Ramin.

## Handle with care

Any proposal that touches the parametric layer is a proposal about the work of
the person Stian reports to. The rewrite is Stian's own idea and is sound on the
merits, but it should be framed as *building on* what Ramin designed and making
it survivable by new engineers — not as replacing it. Worth checking with Stian
before it appears in anything written for others.
