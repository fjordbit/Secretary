## The findings that change the design

- **100 Hz and WinCC Tag Logging are a factor of ten apart.** WinCC's fastest
  archive cycle is 100 ms — 10 Hz. Either the 100 Hz is the PLC scan rate and
  the HMI archives slower, or 10 ms logging is genuinely required and the
  acquisition has to happen in the S7 itself. **This is the gate: every volume,
  storage and licence number moves by 10x depending on the answer.**
- **The right question is "what is the fastest event we must not miss",** not
  "how fast can we log". 10 Hz is generous for running hours and load cycles,
  and may miss a snatch load on a wire rope entirely.
- **PowerTags are an unpriced cost.** Licensed per PLC tag connection in
  bundles; archive tags licensed separately again. ~500 tags on a 50-hoist site
  before anything that is not a hoist. If every site needs a licence upgrade,
  logging via the HMI stops being the cheap option.
- **EG Maintenance is most likely EG MainManager — a CAFM/facility system**, not
  a condition-monitoring platform. It should receive conclusions, not
  measurements. **Inference, not confirmation** — thirty seconds with whoever
  owns the licence settles it, and if it is a different EG product, document 02
  needs redoing.
- **Storage: write locally, replicate to the NAS, back up from there.** SQL
  Server on SMB is supported but Microsoft recommends against it. RAID is not
  backup. Time-series compression is roughly 10x on this data, which is what
  decides whether "log a lot" is affordable.
- **"Tosibox on steroids" is most likely IXON Cloud.** Ewon Flexy the other
  candidate. Prices in document 02. Confirm with whoever said it.
